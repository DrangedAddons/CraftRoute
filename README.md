# CraftRoute

**Level your professions in World of Warcraft: Forever without the guesswork.**

CraftRoute turns the wow-professions.com Forever leveling guides into a live plan. It knows your skill, reads your bags, and tells you what to craft next, what you're missing and what it'll cost. Then it lets you craft it right from the same window.

## What it does

**Follows the guides, step by step.** Covers Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring, First Aid, Cooking and Fishing, plus Fishing + Cooking together. Routes start from your current skill and run to the goal you pick: the next rank, your level's cap, or the end of the guide. Where a guide gives you a choice, you pick the path.

**A Craft tab built for leveling.**
- The recipe to make now, with "Up next" and "After that" beside it.
- Reagent counts for the whole step, coloured by whether you're ready.
- A skill-up colour band and a progress bar for the step.
- A cast bar, and Create / Create All buttons.
- Warnings when a recipe needs a special station, like a Tanning Rack or a Sewing Machine.

**Stops at the steps that aren't crafts.** Training the next rank, a book or quest, supplies to buy, a recipe to learn, fishing to a skill: the route stops and shows what to do, the nearest NPCs with a map pin button, and the cost. It moves on by itself once the game shows it's done, or click Mark as done.

**Makes the parts for you.**
- Short on a component you can make yourself? Click it to open what it's made from and craft it on the spot.
- Chains as deep as they go: scraps → Light → Medium → Heavy Leather.
- Pulls from every profession you have: a blacksmith with Mining can smelt bars, a blacksmith with Leatherworking can make the leather.

**Enchanting made painless.** Pick a target item from your gear or bags, and each click enchants it. The "replace existing enchant?" prompt is answered for you, so you can level without fighting dialogs.

**Knows what you have and what it costs.**
- Materials across your bags, bank, mail and (optionally) alts.
- Auctionator prices, the total cost of what's missing, and a one-click shopping list.
- Buy vendor reagents straight from the Craft tab while a merchant is open.

**Plan for the gear you want.** Tick target recipes (like the Wisdom's Leather set) and they slot into the route at the skill where you can make them.

**Smart tooltips.** Hover any material to see which steps need it, across every profession you've learned.

**Your faction, your look.**
- You only ever see your own faction's trainers and vendors.
- With EllesmereUI installed, you can match its style or pick a Blizzard or Classic look.

## Getting started

Type `/cr` to open CraftRoute.

Optional companions:
- [Auctionator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctionator) for prices and shopping lists.
- [Syndicator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/syndicator) for bank, mail and alt counts.
- TrainerSpells for trainer and recipe vendor locations (map pins on task steps).
- EllesmereUI for the matching themes.

## Notes

Routes follow the beta guides and are kept in step as the guides change. Most guides stop at 225 until the level cap allows more; past that, CraftRoute fills in the cheapest trainer or vendor recipes. Mining, Herbalism and Skinning will be added once Forever guides exist for them.

See `CHANGELOG.md` for what's new.
